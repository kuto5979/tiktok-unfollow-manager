Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

TikTok Unfollow Manager is a desktop application that helps users review and organize accounts they follow on TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may access and how it is handled.

Information from TikTok

If you choose to sign in with TikTok, the application uses TikTok's official authentication system. The information available to the application depends on the permissions granted by TikTok and the user.

How information is used

Information obtained through TikTok authentication is used only to provide the application's requested features, such as identifying the authenticated account and supporting account-management functionality.

Storage and security

The application is designed to minimize the information it stores. Authentication information, when required, should be stored securely and should not be shared with other people. Users should never provide their TikTok password directly to this application.

Third parties

The application uses TikTok's official developer services when TikTok login is enabled. TikTok's own privacy practices also apply to information handled by TikTok.

Data deletion

Users may stop using the application and remove locally stored application data. Where information is held by TikTok, deletion is governed by TikTok's applicable account and privacy controls.

Children

The application is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law or TikTok's services.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about this application, use the contact information provided by the application's developer.

TikTok Unfollow Manager is an independent application and is not operated, sponsored, or endorsed by TikTok.
